# Privacy Policy — Cleared

Last updated: 2026-09-28. Published at https://lechdigital.nl/projects/cleared/privacy/ — keep
the two in sync.

## What this app collects

Nothing. Cleared has no user accounts, no servers, and makes no network requests. It is built
without Android's internet permission, so it cannot open a network connection at all.

## What it stores, and where

In a database inside the app's private storage on your own device: what you are quitting, when
you quit, how much you used and what it cost; your smoking history if you give it; logged slips
(when, what was used, how many, optional trigger); periods of going back to regular use; cravings
ridden out, with the activity used; and daily craving and mood check-ins. None of it is
transmitted. The developer cannot see it and has no copy of it.

## Sharing

No data is shared with anyone, because none is collected. There is no analytics SDK, no crash
reporting, no advertising and no in-app purchases.

The app lists the research behind its claims and links to national stop-smoking services.
Tapping one hands the web address to your browser, which opens it in the normal way. The app
sends nothing, and no information about you travels with that link.

## Your control

Settings → Export as JSON gives you a copy of everything the app holds. Settings → Delete
everything permanently erases it from the device; uninstalling also removes it. There is no
cloud copy, so deletion is final.

## Notifications

If you grant notification permission, reminders are scheduled locally by your device's operating
system. No push server is involved and no device token is transmitted.

## Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children.
